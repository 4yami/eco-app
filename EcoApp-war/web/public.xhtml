Welcome to Eco App

We believe in Reuse — the second of the 3Rs (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle). Eco App connects people within the community so you can find or list pre-loved items, from textbooks to electronics. Buying second-hand reduces waste, saves money, and builds a more sustainable community. Browse what's available, list what you no longer need, and help make Reuse a habit on campus.
